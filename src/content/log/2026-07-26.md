Started *Designing ML Systems*: mapped the book's structure (four recurring questions, five components, five scenarios) and drafted the first post of a reading series. Got stuck on Figure 1-4 until it clicked: with batching, throughput = batch size ÷ latency, so higher latency and higher throughput can coexist — latency becomes a tradeoff you tune, not the inverse of throughput.

Open question: in a trading system, p99 latency describes ~1% of messages — but those cluster at moments of market stress, so how should jitter be specified and tested?

The DiT big picture finally clicked: inside a DiT there is no "image" versus "condition" — there is only the token sequence. Every form of control decomposes into three actions: **add tokens**, **assign them coordinates**, and **decide who is allowed to look at whom**. Claude's refinement, which I'm keeping: a fourth action — global conditions like the timestep never enter as tokens at all, they act through adaLN modulation — plus two axes hiding inside "add tokens": *which weights* process the new tokens (shared vs. per-modality), and *when they exist* (condition tokens are constant across denoising steps, so they can be cached).

SIGMA-GEN reads differently through this frame: single-pass multi-subject identity preservation, with structure guidance anywhere from coarse boxes to pixel-level depth, in one model. Next week's project: map it and its neighbors onto these axes as one table.

Open question: where does SIGMA-GEN place each subject's identity tokens in coordinate space so that multiple identities don't blend?
